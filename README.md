# Protoporphyrin Effects Boosting

Can vitamin C change how protoporphyrin IX (PpIX) reacts to light, and could that help photodynamic therapy? This repository revisits [Danielius Vargonas's 2014 biophysics master's thesis](wiki/thesis/research-overview.md) at Vilnius University and follows the [research that came after it](wiki/research/continuation-and-breakthroughs.md).

The thesis studied model solutions containing PpIX, vitamin C and albumin at different pH levels. Its [findings and proposed mechanisms](wiki/thesis/findings-and-mechanisms.md) offer a starting point; the exact combination's therapeutic benefit remains [unverified](wiki/thesis/limitations-and-open-questions.md).

## Key advances since 2014

Highlights from the [follow-up research through November 19, 2026](wiki/research/continuation-and-breakthroughs.md):

- **Better identification of photoproducts (2026).** Chromatography and tandem mass spectrometry characterized PpIX hydroxyaldehyde and formyl products, advancing beyond spectral resemblance. The thesis's specific product assignment remains open. [Read the photochemistry review](wiki/research/photochemistry-since-the-thesis.md).
- **A new ALA/PpIX therapeutic indication (2026).** The FDA expanded AMELUZ photodynamic therapy to superficial basal cell carcinoma. This is an indication-specific advance with defined response criteria and treatment limits. [Read the clinical evidence](wiki/research/clinical-progress.md).
- **Targeted light-activated treatment (2020).** Japan approved Akalux photoimmunotherapy for selected head and neck cancers, combining antibody targeting with local light activation using a different sensitizer. [Read about this clinical milestone](wiki/research/clinical-progress.md).
- **Ultrasound activation reached human investigation (2025–2026).** Early PpIX sonodynamic studies and controlled trial development extend the field beyond optical illumination; survival benefit remains unproven. [Read about the emerging approach](wiki/research/clinical-progress.md).
- **Vitamin C's clinical picture became clearer (2022–2024).** A small randomized pancreatic-cancer trial reported a survival signal, while colorectal and prostate trials found no overall benefit in their settings. These chemotherapy combinations do not establish PpIX enhancement. [Read the trial comparison](wiki/research/ascorbate-clinical-evidence.md).
