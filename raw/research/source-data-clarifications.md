## AMELUZ response denominators

Original: https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/208081s040lbl.pdf

Locator: section 14.2, PDF pages 13-14, main-target-lesion and subject-response results.

- Main-target-lesion clinical complete responders in the AMELUZ arm: 121/145 (83%).
- Subjects with clinical complete response of all target lesions, main and additional, used for the residual-histology statement: 119.
- Of those 119 subjects, histology confirmed residual sBCC in 26 (22%).
- These are different response definitions; 119 should not be interpreted merely as an unexplained histology subset of the 121 main-lesion responders.

## Sanai sonodynamic study's abstract-level finding

Original: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41296829/

Locator: Abstract, final paragraph.

- The reported pharmacodynamic result is dose-related variation in oxidative-stress and cell-death markers between insonated and untreated portions of each patient's tumor.
- The abstract does not provide the marker effect sizes or a randomized survival comparison. Its authors interpret the study as early evidence supporting further development, while stating that treatment parameters still require optimization for clinical efficacy.
- This finding supports discussion of human pharmacodynamic investigation. It should not be rewritten as proven tumor control or a survival benefit.

## Maršalka photoproduct terminology and mixture context

Original: https://lmaleidykla.lt/ojs/index.php/physics/article/download/4698/3951?inline=1

Locators: section 3.1, discussion of Figure 2; section 3.3; Conclusions.

- Source abbreviation Phl-PhP denotes the product assigned to the phlorin class. It is distinct from a bacteriochlorin assignment.
- The enhanced reduction-product pathway is especially prominent for the acidic mixture containing both BSA and ascorbate. It is not a universal enhancement caused by ascorbate alone.
- The product-class attribution and proposed electron/proton mechanism remain the authors' interpretation; they are not independent structural identification of the thesis product.
